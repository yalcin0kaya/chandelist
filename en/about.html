About Us

Art Bakırcılık Sanayi ve Ticaret A.Ş. was founded in Istanbul in 1984. For more than forty years we have been working brass and copper by hand to produce chandeliers, wall sconces, floor lamps and decorative objects.

Every piece is cut, hammered, soldered and aged in our own workshop. Our antique brass finish is not a plating but a hand-applied patina that deepens over time.

We produce made-to-measure lighting for hotel, restaurant, mosque and residential projects. Single pieces or production runs can be made to your drawings.
