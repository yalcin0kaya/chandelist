Custom Lighting

Every model in our catalogue can be produced in a different size, colour or number of lamp holders. We also realise designs that are entirely your own.

The process: you send a drawing or a reference image, we prepare a technical drawing and a quotation, and production starts once you approve it.

Colour options: antique brass, pearl white, matte black and corten-look oxide. White and gold tones are custom paint finishes, not platings.

For a custom production enquiry, call us on +90 532 668 10 94.
