Side Table

A side table with a round tray top and a slender body. A tray top has two advantages over a flat one: the raised lip keeps a glass or small object from sliding off the edge, and you can lift the table and carry it without spilling what is on it.

The slim, balanced body takes up no room in a tight space. It fits between two chairs, into the gap between a sofa and the wall, or between a bed and a bedside table. Because the weight sits low it does not tip despite looking slight.

Its minimal lines are deliberate. A side table is not the main piece in a room; its job is to make space for a cup and a book. There is no ornament here and it does not ask for attention — what you set on it is what is seen.

The metal body is cut, bent and welded in our Istanbul workshop, and the surface is finished by hand.

It suits living rooms, sitting rooms, bedrooms, reading corners, offices and hotel rooms, giving a comfortable surface for both decorative objects and everyday things.

Available made to measure in other sizes and colours, and in production runs for contract projects.
