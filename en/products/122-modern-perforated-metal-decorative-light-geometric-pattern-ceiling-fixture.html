Table Lamp

A decorative table lamp with minimal lines and a pierced metal body. Lit, the perforated surface casts a texture of light and shadow around it — an effect a bare bulb cannot produce.

That the light is patterned rather than hard decides what this lamp is for: ambient light. Not for working or reading, but for warming a corner, completing a console, being the one source left in the room after the main light goes off.

It runs from a 220 V socket. Being plug-in it needs no wiring; it goes wherever there is a socket and moves to another room whenever you want. In a rented flat, or for anyone who rearranges often, that is a real convenience.

Made in a large and a small size, and the two together read more balanced than either alone.

The metal body is durable and made in our Istanbul workshop.

It suits living rooms, bedrooms, offices, cafés, restaurants and commercial spaces. For interior use.

Bulb not included; a low-wattage warm white lamp shows the pattern most clearly.

Available made to measure in other sizes and colours.
