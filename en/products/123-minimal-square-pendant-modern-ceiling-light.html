A square body with clean, uninterrupted edges — this pendant shows how much presence a restrained form can hold. Nothing decorative, only straight corners, a single-piece metal body and soft light falling from the open base.

At 35 × 41 cm it sits in proportion over a dining table, a kitchen island, a desk or an entrance hall. Used alone it reads as a quiet focal point; hung in pairs or threes over a long table it builds a calm rhythm.

The body is cut, bent and soldered in our Istanbul workshop. The surface is a hand-applied finish rather than a plating, so no two pieces are ever identical.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. The suspension height is adjusted to your ceiling.

Available made to measure in a different size, colour or number of lamp holders, and in production runs for contract projects. Handmade in Istanbul since 1984.
