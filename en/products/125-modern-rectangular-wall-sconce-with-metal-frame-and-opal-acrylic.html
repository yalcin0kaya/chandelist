Modern Rectangular Wall Sconce with Metal Frame and Opal Acrylic

Product code
AP-009
Section
Outdoor, Wall Sconce

A rectangular metal frame and an opal acrylic diffuser. That is the whole of this sconce, and that is the point.

The opal acrylic softens the point source of the lamp and releases it evenly across the full face of the fitting. It does not tire the eye or cast a hard shadow on the wall. The metal frame holds that glowing surface inside one crisp line.

Use it at a bedside, in an evenly spaced run along a corridor, as a rising line up a stairwell wall, or vertically beside a bathroom mirror. It mounts horizontally or vertically and reads correctly either way.

The body is cut and bent in our workshop and the diffuser is made to fit.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours, and in production runs for hotel and contract work. Handmade in Istanbul.
