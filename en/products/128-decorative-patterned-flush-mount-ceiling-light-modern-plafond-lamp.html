A patterned flush-mount ceiling light. It sits directly against the ceiling and does not hang — the only correct answer in a low room. Under a 2.4–2.7 m ceiling a pendant drops to head height and makes the room read lower.

The familiar problem with flush mounts is that they are dull: a plain disc or square says nothing on a ceiling. The geometric pattern worked into this body solves that. Lit, the pattern shows itself overhead and the surface stops being a flat patch of light.

It gives balanced distribution, lighting the whole room without shadow. It is made to serve as the main light.

It suits homes, restaurants, hotels, cafés and commercial spaces, and several can be set at even intervals down a corridor or across a large area.

For ceiling mounting, interior use. Bulb not included.

Available made to measure in other sizes and colours, and in production runs for contract projects. Made in our own Istanbul workshop.
