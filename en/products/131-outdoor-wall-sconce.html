Wall Sconce

The real test of an outdoor sconce is not its first day but its fifth year. This body is made and painted for exterior conditions, with closed edges and soldered joints so that rain, sun and wind-borne dust leave no lasting mark on the surface.

Its lines are plain and let the architecture lead. It sits against the wall and throws light up and down, drawing a vertical line of light on the façade. What is lit is not the fitting but the texture of the wall — stone, render or timber gains depth under it.

It suits building entrances, garden walls, terraces, driveways and poolsides. Placed symmetrically either side of a front door it balances the façade; set at even intervals along a long wall it describes the path.

Maintenance is next to nothing — a wipe with a damp cloth once a year.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop.
