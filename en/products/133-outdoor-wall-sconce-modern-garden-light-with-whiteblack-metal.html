An outdoor sconce should disappear in daylight and be noticed only by its light after dark. The rectangular metal body of this model does exactly that: it sits flat against the wall, throws light up and down, and asks for no attention of its own.

The body is formed from a single piece of bent metal and painted for exterior conditions. Two finishes are offered — white recedes against pale façades, while black draws a crisp line on stone and timber.

It suits garden walls, entrance doors, terraces, poolsides and car-park entries. Set in pairs or threes along a path, it builds an even rhythm of light.

Maintenance is minimal; wiping it down with a damp cloth once a year is enough.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop.
