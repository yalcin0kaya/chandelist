A Moroccan pendant worked in brass and decorative metal. The fretwork across the body is not only ornament; it decides how much light reaches the room and in what pattern. The glass diffuser inside then softens that pattern and takes the hard point source out of the lamp.

So the lighting works in two layers: pattern on the outside, an even brightness within. That gives it both atmosphere and usable light — a balance most decorative Moroccan chandeliers do not manage.

The chain suspension leaves the drop open: long in a high entrance hall, short under a standard ceiling. Chain length is shortened or extended on request.

The surface work is finished by hand in our workshop, with every edge trued individually after cutting.

It suits hotel lobbies, restaurants, dining rooms, living rooms and conservatories, and is at its strongest hung as a group of three at staggered heights in a stairwell.

Bulb not included. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Handmade in Istanbul since 1984.
