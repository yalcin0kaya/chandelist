A plainly drawn pendant, Ø40 cm across and 100 cm tall. There is no ornament here; the whole effect comes from proportion and surface. A tall, narrow volume and an antique brass finish — it asks for nothing else.

In a modern interior, brass is often the one detail that balances the cold materials around it. In a room of concrete, glass, pale walls and grey furniture, this pendant carries the warmth by itself. It stays quiet in a minimal space, but it is felt.

The body is made in metal. The antique brass surface is not a plating; it is built up with a chemical patina and hand sanding and darkens over time, and no two pieces ever tone identically.

Its vertical proportion suits stairwells, double-height living rooms, entrance halls and dining rooms with high ceilings. Over a long kitchen island, a run of two or three sets up a steady rhythm.

Bulb not included. The drop is set to your ceiling height. Available made to measure in a different diameter, height or colour. Handmade in Istanbul.
