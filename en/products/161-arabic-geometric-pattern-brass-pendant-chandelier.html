The geometric pattern cut into the pierced brass body is what this chandelier is really for: it catches the light, breaks it and spreads it across the surfaces of the room. Switched on, the pattern it throws onto the wall carries further than the piece itself.

The body is made of brass and metal. Once the pattern is cut, every edge is trued by hand — without that step the light breaks hard and unevenly along the openings.

The finish is antique brass, achieved with a chemical patina and hand sanding rather than a plating. It darkens over time and takes on its own character with use.

Standing between modern and traditional, it settles as easily into a spare interior as a classical one. It suits living rooms, dining rooms, hotel lobbies and restaurants, and hung as a group of three at staggered heights in a stairwell it becomes the centre of the space.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. Size, colour and chain length can be adjusted to the project. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
