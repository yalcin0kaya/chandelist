A handmade pendant chandelier drawn from the ornamental language of Ottoman architecture. Light filtering through the finely worked metal body fills the room with a warm, soft brightness.

Every piece is made individually and finished in antique brass. The ageing is not a plating; it is worked into the metal itself with a chemical patina and hand sanding. So no two chandeliers carry exactly the same tone — the depth of the patina varies from one application to the next, and it goes on darkening with time.

The working sequence runs like this: the brass sheet is cut, the motifs are opened, every edge is trued by hand, and only then is the body bent and soldered. Skip the trueing and the light breaks unevenly along the openings, leaving the pattern on the wall blurred.

Its timeless design makes it a focal point for dining rooms, hotel lobbies, boutique spaces and high-end living rooms. Dark walls and timber ceilings show the shadow pattern most clearly.

Bulb not included. Chain length shortened or extended on request. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
