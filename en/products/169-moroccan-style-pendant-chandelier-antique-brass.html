A form that comes straight from traditional Moroccan architecture: a body with a hinged door. That detail is functional rather than decorative — you do not have to take the chandelier down to change a lamp, you open the small door, change it and close it again. On a fitting hung from a high ceiling, that difference is felt every year.

Brass, metal and glass are used together. The antique brass surface is not a plating but a patina worked into the metal; it darkens over time and finds its own tone with use.

The body is cut, bent and soldered in our workshop. The hinge and catch of the door are set by hand — fitting work that cannot be done on a production line.

It suits living rooms, dining rooms, hotel lobbies and restaurants. The chain suspension adjusts freely to the ceiling height, and several can be grouped at staggered heights in a stairwell.

Bulb not included. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
