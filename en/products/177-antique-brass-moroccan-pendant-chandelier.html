Moroccan geometry and acrylic panels, working together. The motifs are cut from the metal body and the panels sit between them, so the light passes first through the pattern and then through the panel. That two-stage passage delivers a hard lamp to the room softened and patterned.

Choosing acrylic over glass is deliberate. It is light, it does not shatter, and in high-ceilinged rooms it reduces the load on the suspension. It also carries none of the transport risk glass does.

The finish is antique brass, built up with a chemical patina and hand sanding rather than plated. Every motif edge is worked by hand after cutting.

It suits hotel lobbies, restaurants, high-ceilinged living rooms and entrance halls. Hung as a group of three at staggered heights, it fills the vertical volume of a tall space.

Bulb not included. Chain length is shortened or extended on request. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Handmade in Istanbul.
