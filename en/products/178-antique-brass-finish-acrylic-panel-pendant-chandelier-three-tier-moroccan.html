Chandelier, Lantern Chandelier

A body that widens upward in three tiers, with pointed-arch window motifs on every level. The pointed arch is a structural element of Islamic architecture — a form that carries load outward — and it is brought here into lighting. The windows do the same work: they spread the light not in one direction but evenly from every arch around the piece.

Because the tiers widen as they rise, the light forms a cone narrowing downward. The area beneath is clearly lit while everything around it stays patterned and soft.

Acrylic panels are set behind the arches. At this scale acrylic is a question of weight and transport, and it also spreads light more evenly than glass.

The finish is antique brass — a patina worked into the metal rather than a plating, and it darkens over time.

It suits hotel lobbies, restaurants, art galleries, and the central lighting of large living spaces.

Bulb not included. Chain length set on request. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
