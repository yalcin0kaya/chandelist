A conical pendant hung on a brass chain. The cone widens downward, spreading light sideways while keeping the area beneath it broad. Over a dining table, where several people sit together, that gives a brightness that reaches the whole surface.

The suspension chain is brass too — the same material as the body. On most chandeliers the chain is black or chrome and reads out of the picture; here it is a visible continuation of the piece. An unbroken line of brass runs from ceiling to body, which makes the fitting look more whole under a high ceiling.

The geometric motifs are opened by hand and every edge trued individually. The finish is antique brass — a patina worked into the metal rather than a plating, darkening over time. The chain ages at the same rate, so no difference in tone develops between them.

It suits hotel lobbies, restaurants, dining rooms and living rooms. Chain length is shortened or extended on request.

Bulb not included. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Handmade in Istanbul.
