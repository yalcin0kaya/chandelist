A layered pendant, Ø50 cm across and 40 cm tall. The body is not one piece but a set of metal hoops stacked over each other. Each hoop carries its own pattern and breaks the light once more — what leaves the source passes through several layers before it reaches the room.

That construction gives the pattern on the wall far more depth than a single-layer chandelier. As the patterns overlap they close in some places and open in others, so what you see is not fixed but shifts with the angle you look from.

The hoops are bent separately, and the spacing between them is set by hand before soldering. That spacing is the critical dimension — it is what determines the pattern.

The finish is antique brass — a patina worked into the metal rather than a plating; it darkens over time and tones slightly differently on each hoop.

It suits hotel lobbies, bedrooms, dining tables and living rooms. At 40 cm tall it works comfortably under standard ceilings too.

Bulb not included. Chain length set on request. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Handmade in Istanbul.
