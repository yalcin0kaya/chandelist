A spherical body, 40 × 40 cm, worked in filigree with glass panels set between the strands. The sphere is the only form without a front: seen from any side it shows the same silhouette and gives light equally in every direction. Over a table standing in the middle of a room, or a reception desk read from all sides, that matters.

Filigree means drawing the metal into wire and weaving it into pattern — not cutting, but bending and soldering each strand. The glass panels seat within that weave, so the light passes first through glass and then out through the openings in the filigree.

The body is antique brass; the chain and fixings are black metal. The surface is not a plating but a patina worked into the metal, and it darkens over time.

It suits hotel lobbies, reception areas, living rooms, dining rooms and entrance halls. Spheres do not clash when grouped — hung as three at staggered heights they stay orderly.

Bulb not included. Chain length set on request. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop.
