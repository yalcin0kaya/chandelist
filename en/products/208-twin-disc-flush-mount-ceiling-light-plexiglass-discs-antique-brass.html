A modern flush mount ceiling light built from two overlapping translucent plexiglass discs carried on an antique brass bar. Light rises from the ribbed brass cones beneath each disc and washes back off the plates, so the room fills with a soft, even glow instead of a glare point. At only 16 cm deep, the low profile suits ceilings where a pendant will not fit — hallways, entryways, bedrooms and studies. The metalwork is made in our Istanbul workshop and the brass is hand-antiqued. Measures 57 × 36 cm with Ø36 cm discs. Suited to living rooms, corridors, hotel rooms and boutique interiors.
