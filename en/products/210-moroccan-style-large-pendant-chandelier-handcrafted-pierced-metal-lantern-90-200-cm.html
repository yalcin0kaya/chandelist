A monumental Moroccan lantern chandelier, hand-pierced from solid metal by craftsmen using techniques passed down through generations. Measuring 90 cm in diameter and 200 cm in height, it is built for spaces that need presence: hotel lobbies, villa entrances, stairwells, restaurants and double-height living rooms.

Thousands of hand-cut perforations turn the light into a canopy of teardrop shadows across walls and ceilings. Unlit, the piece reads as a dark, richly textured sculpture. The three-part silhouette — domed crown, fluted central drum and tapered finial — follows the classic Moroccan and Ottoman lantern form.
