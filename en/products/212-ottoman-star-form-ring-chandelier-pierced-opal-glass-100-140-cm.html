Chandelier

A three-tier Ottoman chandelier that reads as an eight-point star when seen from below. A wide carved ring sits at the top, a band of hexagonal panels at the middle, and a pointed domed lantern beneath.

The three tiers do three different jobs. The upper ring throws light onto the ceiling and the dome above — in a mosque or a large hall the ceiling itself is lit. The middle band sends light outward to the walls. The lower lantern brings it straight down to the floor. A single-tier fitting can do only one of the three.

Every surface is covered in hand-worked rumi and palmette carving. The rumi is the curving animal motif of Seljuk and Ottoman ornament; the palmette is a fan-shaped leaf. Used together, the pattern carries both movement and order.

Opal glass panels behind the carving filter the light into a warm brightness that never dazzles. Unlit the piece reads dark and textured; lit, it fills the ceiling with pattern.

Finish: antique copper Sizes: Ø100 cm × 100 cm and Ø140 cm × 190 cm (made to measure on request) Suited to: mosques, prayer halls, hotel lobbies, villa halls, restaurants, large reception rooms

Bulbs not included. The suspension and fixing point are engineered to the size. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
