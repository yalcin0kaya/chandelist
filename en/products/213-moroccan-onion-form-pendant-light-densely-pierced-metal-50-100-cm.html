A single-piece pierced metal pendant in the classic Moroccan onion silhouette. 50 cm across, 100 cm tall including the chain.

The body is worked with thousands of tiny triangular perforations. Lit, it scatters the whole room with pinpricks of light; from below it opens into a rose window radiating from the centre. A small dome and drop finial finish the base.

Colour: Antique brass patina Best for: Above a dining table, entrance halls, bedrooms, cafés, hotel rooms, stairwells

Hung in pairs or threes over a dining table, the effect is striking.
