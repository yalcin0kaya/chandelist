Chandelier

Two stepped brass drums, one set within the other. The upper is wide, the lower narrow, and each is pierced with a different pattern — an open teardrop weave above, a much finer scrolling vine below. Two distinct densities of cutwork on one piece.

An opal glass disc closes the base. It conceals the lamp entirely and throws an even, glare-free light downward, while what escapes through the tiers washes the ceiling in pattern. Illumination and shadow, working at the same time.

Overall depth is 35 cm — the way to bring Moroccan cutwork into rooms too low for a pendant.

Antique brass patina. Pierced by hand in our workshop.
