Chandelier

A sphere, cut on the diagonal and slid apart. The crescent gap left between the halves is the only place light escapes.

The outward faces are hammered by hand — thousands of small dimples that catch and scatter the light. The inward faces are left smooth, so the lamp gathers there and leaves through the crescent as a hard, clean line. The sphere reads as closed, and lights the room anyway.

The bulb is invisible from every angle. It can hang just above eye level over a table without glare.

Ø20 cm. Chain made to the room. Also produced with a smooth rather than hammered surface.

Antique brass patina. The sphere is raised by hand. At its best hung in threes, or in fives at staggered heights.
