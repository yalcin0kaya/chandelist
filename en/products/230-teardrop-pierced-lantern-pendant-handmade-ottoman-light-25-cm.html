A pierced teardrop body above, a turned solid collar at the waist, a second pierced bowl below. The collar separates them and is the only unbroken surface on the piece.

Both bodies are cut end to end with vine and rumi motifs. Light leaves upward from the top and downward from the base, while the solid collar passes none at all. The result on the wall is not one patch of light but two separate rings of pattern with a band of dark between them — which makes the lantern read far larger than it hangs.

Ø25 cm, approximately 50 cm tall. Made to any size.

Antique brass patina. The same model is also made as a wall sconce.
