A sheet was taken, folded, and left where it folded. That is the whole of the Origami Planter — no applied ornament, no moulded imitation texture, only the folds of the metal itself.

FORM Twenty triangular faces catch the light at different angles through the day. In the morning one face lifts; by midday three do; in the evening the shadows sharpen and the planter reads as a different mass altogether. A still object that changes all day — that comes from the folds. From the corner it is a cut gem; from the front, a quiet cube.

MATERIAL AND MAKING The body is made from 1.5 mm aluminium sheet. Each triangular face is cut, folded and welded to the next; what comes out reads as a single, seamless volume. Both sizes are one piece — no assembly, no screws, nothing to put together. It is used exactly as it comes out of the box.

FINISH Electrostatic powder coating, oven-cured. Because powder coat bonds electrostatically to the body and hardens in the oven, it is far tougher than ordinary paint: it does not scratch off, fade in sun or blister in damp.

WHY ALUMINIUM Aluminium does not rust. It is far lighter than concrete, stone or terracotta at the same size — one person can move it even when planted. That makes it practical on balconies, terraces and roof gardens with limited load capacity. It does not need to come indoors for winter: it will not crack in frost the way terracotta does.

DRAINAGE AND INNER LINER There is a drainage hole in the base, so water does not collect in the body. The planter is used with an inner liner (bucket): the plant is potted into the liner and the liner sits inside the planter. This lets you swap the plant with the season, lift the liner out without emptying the planter, and keep soil from sitting directly against the aluminium.

OPENING AND PLANTING The top opening is a 22.5 × 22.5 cm square. Because the body widens below the rim, the volume inside is noticeably greater than the opening suggests. It suits single-stem trees and rounded forms: olive, lemon, bay, boxwood, bonsai, cacti and succulents. (The plant is not included.)

DIMENSIONS Widest point (corner to corner): Ø45 cm Height: 32 cm Opening (internal): 22.5 × 22.5 cm Material: 1.5 mm aluminium Finish: Electrostatic powder coating Body: One piece, welded Drainage: Hole in base Use: With an inner liner (bucket)

COLOURS Matt Black, Matt White and Oxide (Corten look). All three are electrostatic powder coating on the aluminium body. The corten-look finish is not real weathering steel; the oxide texture is painted on. The practical benefit: real corten bleeds in the rain and permanently stains the stone, timber or concrete beneath it — this planter does not.

WHERE IT WORKS Entrances and doorways, terraces and balconies, roof gardens, garden paths, poolsides, hotel and restaurant entrances, shop windows and showrooms. A pair frames a doorway symmetrically; a single one anchors a corner. It works indoors too.

CARE Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners, steel wool or solvents. Powder coating needs no maintenance and should not be polished. It can stay outside through winter; just keep the drainage hole clear.

MADE In our own Istanbul workshop, making since 1984.
