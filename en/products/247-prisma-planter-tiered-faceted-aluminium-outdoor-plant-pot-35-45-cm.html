A hexagonal ring, then another, then another. The Prisma Planter comes out of repeating that one rule: each tier carries its own triangles, the next sits on top rotated by half a step, and as the body rises the faces fall into each other's shadow.

FORM Every tier has six triangular faces. Through the day the light travels up them; in the morning one band lifts while the next stays in shadow, at midday they all open, in the evening the lines between the tiers sharpen. A still object that changes all day comes from this.

The middle size. Three tiers lift the body off the floor and carry the plant up toward eye level. One anchors a corner; a pair frames a passage. It is the most balanced height for lemon, myrtle, boxwood and medium foliage.

MATERIAL AND MAKING The body is made from 1.5 mm aluminium sheet. Each tier is a hexagonal ring: the triangular faces are cut one by one, folded and welded to each other. The tiers are then stacked and welded together into a single body — they do not come apart and nothing needs assembling. Weld lines are ground back by hand until only the line of the tiers remains.

FINISH Electrostatic powder coating, oven cured. Because the powder bonds electrostatically and hardens in the oven, it is far tougher than ordinary paint: it does not scratch easily, fade in the sun, blister in damp or flake in winter.

WHY ALUMINIUM Aluminium does not rust. It is far lighter than concrete, stone or terracotta at the same size; one person can move it even when planted. That makes it easy on balconies, terraces and roof gardens with limited load capacity. It does not need to come indoors for winter — it will not crack in frost the way terracotta does.

DRAINAGE AND INDOOR USE There is a drainage hole in the base, so water does not collect in the body. Indoors we recommend using an inner liner (a bucket or plastic pot) that drops inside rather than planting directly into it; water never reaches the floor and the plant can be swapped without emptying the planter.

OPENING AND PLANTING The top opening is a hexagon of roughly 17.5 × 17.5 cm. Because the body widens below the rim, the volume inside is greater than the opening suggests. It suits single-stem trees and rounded forms: olive, lemon, bay, boxwood, bonsai, cacti and succulents. (The plant is not included.)

COLOURS Matt White and Matt Black. Both are electrostatic powder coating, not a plating. Matt white belongs to the same family as stone and travertine — it disappears against a pale floor and lets the plant lead. Matt black does the opposite: it reduces the form to a sharp silhouette and makes green read brighter. The lines between the tiers show more strongly in black and more softly in white.

WHERE IT WORKS Entrances and doorways, terraces and balconies, roof gardens, garden paths, poolsides, hotel and restaurant entrances, shop windows and showrooms. The three heights together build a stepped composition; one on its own anchors a corner. It works indoors too.

CARE Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners, steel wool or solvents. Powder coating needs no maintenance and should not be polished. It can stay outside through winter; just keep the drainage hole clear.
