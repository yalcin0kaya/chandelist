Chandelier

A light that sits against the ceiling instead of hanging from it. It belongs where a pendant will not fit — a low hallway, a bathroom, a dressing room. The body is only 14 cm deep, yet the fluted brass band and the soft curve of the opal glass give it a presence no ordinary ceiling fixture has.

The flutes run vertically and wrap the band without interruption. When the lamp is lit, their crests catch the light while the grooves stay in shadow — the metal never reads as a single flat tone. The antique brass patina deepens that rhythm: dark in the hollows, bright along the ridges.

The glass is opal and frosted. It hides the bulb and does not glare. The light it throws downward is even and soft — strong enough to carry a corridor end to end, measured enough to sit above a bathroom mirror.

FORM AND CRAFT Body: Brass — vertically fluted band with plain collars top and bottom Diffuser: Opal frosted glass with a gently domed underside Finish: Antique brass patina — not paint or plating, but a finish worked into the metal itself Each flute is cut individually; the collars are drawn separately and soldered to the body Made in our own Istanbul workshop, by the same method since 1984

DIMENSIONS Diameter: Ø30 cm Height: 14 cm Drop: None — mounts flush to the ceiling

LIGHT AND ELECTRICAL 2 × E27 sockets Suitable for LED, filament or energy-saving bulbs Bulbs not included Dimmable when used with a dimmable bulb 220–240 V

INSTALLATION Mounts directly via the ceiling plate to a junction box or concrete slab The glass diffuser releases on a bayonet collar — no need to remove the fixture to change a bulb Designed for low ceilings, hallways, entries, bathrooms, dressing rooms, stair landings and small bedrooms

CARE Wipe with a dry or lightly damp soft cloth. No abrasive cleaners, polish or metal brightener. The patina deepens over time; this is the material ageing, not a defect.

MADE Handmade in our own workshop in Istanbul. Available as a bespoke order in the size, finish and socket count you require.
