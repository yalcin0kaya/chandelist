Chandelier

A fixture made to stand over a long table, a kitchen island or a bar counter. A 125 cm rectangular brass cage, ten bulbs in a row inside it, and a curtain of clear glass rods around them.

The rods run vertically and cover all four faces. When the bulbs are lit, the light passes through them, refracts in each curve and leaves as fine vertical lines. Up close you see hundreds of separate threads of light; from across the room, one warm band.

The brass frame is kept thin. Two profiles top and bottom, four corner posts — the rest is glass and light. The structure carries a 125 cm span without ever looking heavy.

The underside is open. Light falls straight onto the counter while a softer halo spreads sideways through the glass. Together they give a fixture that lights a working surface and sets the mood of the room at the same time.

FORM AND CRAFT Body: Brass — rectangular box cage, profile frame, four corner posts Curtain: Clear fluted glass rods — an unbroken row on all four faces, set one by one Suspension: Two brass chains and a 125 cm brass canopy Finish: Antique brass patina — not paint or plating, but a finish worked into the metal itself Frame corners are soldered and dressed by hand; the rods are fitted individually in the workshop Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984

DIMENSIONS Length: 125 cm Depth: 35 cm Body height: 30 cm Canopy: 125 × 8 cm Overall drop: Set by the chain — shortened or extended on request

LIGHT AND ELECTRICAL 10 × E27 sockets Suitable for LED, filament or energy-saving bulbs Filament bulbs give the best result behind a clear glass rod curtain Bulbs not included Dimmable when used with dimmable bulbs 220–240 V

WHERE IT WORKS Designed for kitchen islands, long dining tables, bar counters, boardroom tables, hotel lobbies and café counters. Hang 75–85 cm above a counter; 80–90 cm above a dining table.

CARE Wipe the brass with a dry or lightly damp soft cloth. Clean the glass rods in place with a soft brush or microfibre cloth. No abrasive cleaners, polish or metal brightener. The patina deepens over time.
