Chandelier

A seventy-centimetre brass ring, hundreds of glass rods standing upright around it, six bulbs inside. The drum is a simple form; what carries it is the proportion and the way the glass behaves with light.

The rods wrap the whole circumference without a break. Each stands vertically, held top and bottom by a brass collar. Lit from within, every rod becomes a line of its own — the curtain is not one surface but hundreds of threads of light side by side. Move around it and a different set of rods catches fire; the fixture stays still, its appearance does not.

A second, smaller ring sits below. It braces the structure and softens the edge of the light falling through. Above, a four-armed cross frame takes four steel cables that distribute the load evenly. The central chain and its fluted brass sphere are the only ornament the suspension allows itself.

The underside is open, so light reaches the table directly, while the light that spreads outward is filtered through glass — bright enough over a dining table, never glaring, and warm against the walls.

FORM AND CRAFT Body: Brass — upper and lower rings, four-armed cross frame, inner second ring Curtain: Clear fluted glass rods — an unbroken row around the circumference, set one by one Suspension: Four steel cables, brass chain and a fluted brass sphere canopy Finish: Antique brass patina — not paint or plating, but a finish worked into the metal itself Each ring is rolled from a single length, soldered at the joint and dressed by hand; the rods are fitted individually Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984

DIMENSIONS Diameter: Ø70 cm Body height: 32 cm Overall drop: Set by the chain and cables — shortened or extended on request

LIGHT AND ELECTRICAL 6 × E27 sockets Suitable for LED, filament or energy-saving bulbs Filament bulbs give the best result behind a clear glass rod curtain Bulbs not included Dimmable when used with dimmable bulbs 220–240 V

WHERE IT WORKS Designed for dining tables, entrance halls, living rooms, hotel lobbies, cafés and restaurants. Hang 80–90 cm above a round dining table; in an entrance hall leave at least 220 cm of clearance from the floor.

CARE Wipe the brass with a dry or lightly damp soft cloth. Clean the glass rods in place with a soft brush or microfibre cloth. No abrasive cleaners, polish or metal brightener. The patina deepens over time; this is the material ageing, not a defect.
