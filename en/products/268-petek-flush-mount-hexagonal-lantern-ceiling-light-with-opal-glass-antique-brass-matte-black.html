A hexagonal lantern, set against the ceiling. The sloping shoulders and pointed finial of a classic street lantern are kept; the chain and the tall body are gone, and what remains rests straight against the plaster. A Ø60 cm surface, only 28 cm deep.

The frame is built from slim profiles. Each panel sits in a frame of its own: a horizontal collar above, six angled faces below, meeting at a single point. The metal draws the structure, the glass fills it — the ratio between the two is this fixture's whole character.

The glass is opal and frosted. It hides the bulb; the light scatters as it passes through, and every face of the hexagon reads at a slightly different brightness. Even unlit, the matte white planes give the ceiling volume — the fixture has a form whether or not it is switched on.

Made in two finishes: antique brass and matte black. The brass reads warm and traditional, the black graphic and sharp — the same form translated into two different rooms.

FORM AND CRAFT Body: Metal — hexagonal profile frame, upper collar, six angled side panels, finial below Glass: Opal frosted panels — each cut separately and seated in its own frame Finish: Antique brass patina (worked into the metal itself) or matte black powder coat Frame corners are soldered and dressed by hand; the glazing beads are fitted one by one Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984

DIMENSIONS Diameter: Ø60 cm (point to point) Height: 28 cm Drop: None — mounts flush to the ceiling

LIGHT AND ELECTRICAL 3 × E27 sockets Suitable for LED, filament or energy-saving bulbs The opal glass conceals the bulbs and gives an even, glare-free light Bulbs not included Dimmable when used with dimmable bulbs 220–240 V

WHERE IT WORKS Designed for entrance halls, vestibules, corridors, stair landings, covered porches and colonnades. It gives scale to large rooms with low ceilings, where a hanging fixture will not fit.

CARE Wipe with a dry or lightly damp soft cloth. No abrasive cleaners, polish or metal brightener. The brass patina deepens over time; this is the material ageing, not a defect.
