A halo standing beside the chair.

The same shade as the table lamp, lifted onto a slender 160 cm stem. The pleated parasol — antique brass outside, mirror inside — is 40 cm across and sits above the head of anyone seated, throwing the light down and outward. The ring-chain gallery at the rim breaks that light into separate rings; lit in the evening, it leaves a turning pattern on the wall and the floor.

Three candle arms gather just beneath the shade. The stem is a single slim tube with a collar at its midpoint; the base is wide and round, and keeps the lamp from tipping. The whole surface is aged by hand.

Dimensions: 40 cm diameter, 160 cm high. Beside a chair, at the end of a sofa, in a reading corner, an entrance hall, a bedroom. It works as a reading light, though its real task is to set the character of the room.

Made to order. Height, diameter, colour and quantity change on request; metal and electrical fittings are specified per order.

The rest of the series: a wall sconce, a table lamp and a four-arm chandelier.
