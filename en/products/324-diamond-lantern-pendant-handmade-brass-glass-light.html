Lantern Chandelier, Single Pendant Light

A handmade lantern pendant that carries the classic lantern silhouette into a modern, diamond-cut geometry. The body widens from a hexagonal upper crown and draws back in again, meeting along a single profile line, with every facet between filled by clear flat glass. Behind the panels, four candle-form light sources throw light into the room without breaking it; unlit, the brass frame and the glass read as an object in their own right.

The body is cut, folded and soldered sheet by sheet in our workshop; the corner profiles are trued by hand and each pane is seated individually. The finish is hand-applied rather than moulded, so the antique tone of every piece is slightly its own — no two are identical.

Dimensions: 40 cm wide × 40 cm deep × 38 cm body height. Ceiling canopy Ø12 × 2.5 cm, hanging ring Ø3.5 cm. The chain is adjustable up to a maximum of 150 cm; it is shortened to your ceiling height and can be produced longer on request.

Colour options: Antique Brass, Black and Pearl White. Black and Pearl White are custom-mixed paint finishes, not a plating; the surface stays matte and even and will not flake over time. Antique brass is a hand-applied finish with real depth.

It suits entrance halls, kitchen islands, over a dining table, stairwells and long corridors — alone or in aligned pairs and threes. In high-ceilinged rooms the chain is extended to match the scale of the space.
