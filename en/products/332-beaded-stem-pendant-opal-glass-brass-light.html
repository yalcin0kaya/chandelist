Chandelier, Single Pendant Light

The stem descending from the ceiling is made of brass beads threaded one above another. Each bead is turned individually, sanded by hand and fitted to the next; as the string runs down it becomes not a plain tube but a line with a rhythm. At its end sits opal glass.

The light sits inside the glass. Opal does not pass light straight through — it scatters it inside and gives it out evenly, so the bulb is never visible from any angle and never catches the eye. The brass string does not reflect; it simply holds the glass above it. What is lit is the glass, while the stem stays a dark line.

Two glass forms are produced, both Ø15 cm: • Sphere — fully round, the classic stance • Oval — flattened horizontally, softer and more modern

The stem length is set by the number of beads; simply state your ceiling height when ordering. Taking several at different lengths and hanging them staggered is how this piece is really meant to be used: over a dining table, along a kitchen island, in a stairwell or in a tall lobby, hung by the dozen at varying heights, it reads as a set of lights suspended in the air.

Colour: Antique brass, hand-applied with real depth; it darkens where the beads meet and lifts across their crowns.
