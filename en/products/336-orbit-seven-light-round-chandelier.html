Chandelier

The Orbit head is built from three parts: a fluted brass body above, a Ø30 cm acrylic disc held horizontally at its middle, and a small base below that carries the same fluting. The disc rings the body like an orbit — which is where the name comes from.

The flutes are turned on the lathe step by step rather than in one pass; each ring is taken off with its own cut. That is why the surface glints in bands under the light. The brass is then aged by hand, darkening in the grooves and lifting on the crests of the flutes.

The light sits inside the body and is never visible from any angle. The acrylic disc draws the light in first, then spreads it to its edge at an even density: the disc glows without glaring. The light that falls carries a clean circle onto the table below.

Antique brass. Drop length is set to your ceiling height.

In the seven-light model the heads descend from a single round brass canopy at different heights. The discs are offset rather than stacked, so they read between one another; seen from below they appear as overlapping circles of light.

It suits an entrance hall, a round dining table, a stair landing or the centre of a living room. The same canopy is built with 3, 4, 5 or 7 lights, and the count and the height differences are set to the room.

Dimensions: each disc Ø30 cm. Canopy diameter and drops made to order.
