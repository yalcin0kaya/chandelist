Chandelier

The Elara body is a string of brass beads threaded one above another. Each bead is turned individually, sanded by hand and fitted to the next; as the string runs down it becomes not a plain tube but a line with a rhythm. At its end sits Ø15 cm opal glass.

The light sits inside the glass. Opal does not pass light straight through; it scatters it inside and gives it out evenly, so the bulb is never visible from any angle. The brass string does not reflect, it simply holds the glass: what is lit is the glass, while the stem stays a dark line.

Each head's length is set by its number of beads; simply state your ceiling height when ordering.

Colour: Antique brass, hand-applied; it darkens where the beads meet and lifts across their crowns.

Five heads descend from a round canopy in a tight cluster, the glasses offset so they read between one another. For the centre of a living room or a wide entrance hall.
