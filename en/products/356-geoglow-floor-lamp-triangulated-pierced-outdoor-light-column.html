Floor Lamp, Outdoor

A column with a triangular section. The body twists slightly as it rises, so no face ever looks like another — the form changes as you walk around it.

The surface is not flat: interlocking triangles are laser-cut and the gaps between them left open. The opal diffuser inside softens the light first, then releases it through these triangular openings. The result is not a lamp that lights a space but a volume that glows.

At night the body reads as a cage of light; the edges of the triangles stay dark and their centres shine. By day the metal texture and its pierced pattern make a sculptural object in their own right.

The triangular base seats the column securely, its weight low against tipping. The paint is a custom finish prepared for outdoor conditions: it will not fade in the sun or rust in the rain.

Colour options: gold, beige and black.

For terraces, poolsides, garden corners, hotel lobbies and restaurant entrances. In pairs or in threes at different heights the effect is stronger.
