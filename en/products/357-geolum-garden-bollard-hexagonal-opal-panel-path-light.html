A slender hexagonal column. All six faces are closed with opal panels; the light leaves evenly in every direction and glares in none.

Its crown is cut like a gem: triangular facets interlock and taper at the top. This is not only form — it throws rain clear and stops light escaping to the sky.

The slim black frame leaves a sharp line between the opal panels. At night those lines reveal the lantern's skeleton; the piece reads not as one bright mass but as a drawn volume.

The bulb is never visible. Along a walkway, with several set in a row, that matters: the line of light stays even, it does not tire the eye and it does not spill into a neighbour's window.

The base has a floor flange and is anchored into concrete or stone. The paint is a custom finish prepared for outdoor conditions.

For garden paths, villa entrances, estate walkways, hotel grounds and poolsides.
