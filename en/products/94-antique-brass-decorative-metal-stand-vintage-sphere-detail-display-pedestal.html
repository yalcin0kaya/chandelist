This distinguished decorative metal stand features a beautifully aged antique brass finish, each piece uniquely patinated to create an authentic vintage character. The elegant sphere-detailed body and balanced proportions make it far more than an accessory — it is a statement piece in its own right. The generous top surface provides versatile display space for candles, vases, decorative objects, and curated tabletop arrangements. Its timeless aesthetic harmonizes effortlessly with modern, classic, and rustic interior styles. Crafted from durable metal with a lasting antique brass coating, this stand brings sophistication to living rooms, console tables, sideboards, and retail displays.

The antique brass surface is not a plating. It is built up with a chemical patina and hand sanding, so every piece tones slightly differently, and it goes on darkening with use.

Two or three stands at different heights read better together than one alone.

Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
