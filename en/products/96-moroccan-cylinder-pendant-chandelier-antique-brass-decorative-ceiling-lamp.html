Inspired by the timeless architectural motifs of Moroccan design, this cylinder pendant chandelier is a masterwork of decorative metalwork. Crafted in metal with a hand-finished antique brass patina, the cylindrical body features intricate geometric and floral cut-out patterns that cast enchanting shadow lattices across ceilings and walls when illuminated. The warm golden tones create an intimate, exotic ambiance that elevates dining rooms, hotel lobbies, restaurants, and living rooms into atmospheric retreats. Each piece is individually handmade, making every chandelier a unique work of functional art.

The cut-outs are opened one at a time and every edge is trued by hand after cutting. A rough edge breaks the light unevenly and leaves the shadow on the wall blurred — it is the step that decides whether the pattern reads crisply.

The antique brass surface is not a plating but a patina worked into the metal itself, and it darkens over time.

Bulb not included. Chain length set on request. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
