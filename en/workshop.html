Our Workshop

Our workshop is in Bahçelievler, Istanbul. Brass sheet is cut, bent, hammered and soldered here.

On pierced and fretwork models the patterns are CNC cut and the edges are then finished by hand. Hammered surfaces are raised entirely with a hammer.

The ageing is done with a chemical patina and hand sanding, which is why no two pieces are ever identical.
