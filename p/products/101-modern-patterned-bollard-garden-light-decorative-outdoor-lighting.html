Modern Patterned Bollard Garden Light – Decorative Outdoor Lighting

Price on request — request a quote.

A vertical, patterned garden bollard. A vertical form takes less ground in a garden than a horizontal fitting and does not disappear into planting; it stands as a slim line and never interrupts a view.

The pattern on the body decides how the light lands. A plain bollard leaves a uniform ring on the ground; a patterned one lays a patterned trace along the path. Walking through a garden in the evening, that is what you actually notice.

It uses a GU10 lamp holder, which makes changing the lamp easy and gives a wide choice. At 9 W it draws little while still giving usable light.

Made in our own workshop in Turkey.

It suits garden paths, driveways, terrace edges, poolsides and shared areas in housing developments. Set 1.5–2 metres apart along a route, the units merge into one continuous band of light.

Available made to measure in other sizes and colours, and in production runs for contract projects.
