Handmade Brass Lighting / Floor Lamps

Modern Perforated Metal Decorative Light – Geometric Pattern Ceiling Fixture

Price on request — request a quote.

A table lamp with a pierced metal body. The perforations do not hold the light in, they let it through — switched on, the lamp lays a pattern of points across the table and the wall behind it. What is lit is not the surface but that pattern.

It is made as atmosphere lighting rather than task light; this is not a reading or working lamp. Its real effect shows when it is the only source left on after the main light goes off in the evening.

It runs from a 220 V socket. Because it is plug-in there is no installation, no wiring and no electrician — it goes anywhere there is a socket, and moves with you.

A large and a small size are offered. Used side by side, the difference in height creates movement on the table and reads better than a single size alone.

The durable metal body is made for long service, and the modern, minimal form works with both classical and contemporary interiors.

It suits living rooms, bedrooms, offices, cafés, restaurants and commercial spaces. For interior use.

Bulb not included; a low-wattage warm white lamp shows the pattern most clearly. Made in our own Istanbul workshop.
