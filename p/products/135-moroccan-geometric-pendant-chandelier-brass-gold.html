Handmade Brass Lighting / Chandeliers

Moroccan Geometric Pendant Chandelier - Brass Gold

Price on request — request a quote.

The brass body is pierced with geometric cuts, so the light never arrives directly — it passes through the pattern first. What reaches the room is a lace of light across the walls and ceiling, the effect Moroccan and Ottoman lighting has relied on for centuries.

Brass, metal and glass work together here. Each cut is worked into the body individually and the edges are finished by hand. Against the lamp, the warmth of the brass turns golden.

It suits rooms where evening light matters — living rooms, bedrooms, restaurants, hotel lobbies, conservatories. In a high entrance hall it holds the space on its own.

The finish is antique brass: a hand-applied patina rather than a plating, and it deepens with time. Matte black and pearl white are available as custom paint finishes.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. Size and chain length can be adjusted to the project. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
