Antique Brass Pendant Chandelier – 40 cm Acrylic Detail

Price on request — request a quote.

A vertical body, Ø40 cm across and 100 cm tall. The proportion is what sets this piece apart from most pendants: it occupies height rather than width, which is why it holds a narrow, tall volume — a stairwell, an entrance hall, a double-height living room — on its own.

The metal body carries an antique brass finish, while the acrylic detail softens the lamp and releases it evenly through the form. Using acrylic instead of glass keeps the piece light and removes any risk of breakage in transit.

The antique brass surface is not a plating but a hand-applied patina, and it darkens with time.

Use it singly over a dining table, in a pair over a long one, or as a group of three at staggered heights in a stairwell. The drop is set to your ceiling height.

Bulb not included; contact us for the lamp holder type and the lamp we recommend. Available made to measure in a different diameter, height or colour. Handmade in Istanbul since 1984.
