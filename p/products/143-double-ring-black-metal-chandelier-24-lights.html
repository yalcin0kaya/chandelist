Double Ring Black Metal Chandelier - 24 Lights

Price on request — request a quote.

Two concentric rings and twenty-four candle lights. On a chandelier of this type the number of rings decides more than its size — it decides how the light is distributed: the upper ring lights the ceiling and the top of the walls, the lower one the table and the seating. A single-tier fitting cannot make that distinction.

Twenty-four lights each run at low power. That gives a far softer brightness than one strong source: shadows are not hard but filled in from several directions. It is the reason classical candle chandeliers have always been chosen.

The body is built in black metal and iron. Black sets up a contrast with the crystal elements; bright pieces read far more clearly against a dark ground. A brass or gold-toned body cannot produce the same contrast.

It is made for large rooms: living rooms, dining rooms, hotel lobbies, conference rooms and villa entrances. It needs both ceiling height and floor area.

Bulbs not included. Size, arm count and colour can be changed per project. Made in our own Istanbul workshop since 1984.
