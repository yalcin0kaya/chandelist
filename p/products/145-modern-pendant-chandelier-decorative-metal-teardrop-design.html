Modern Pendant Chandelier - Decorative Metal Teardrop Design

Price on request — request a quote.

A teardrop-form pendant in pierced metal. The teardrop narrows towards the bottom, which gathers the light to a point instead of spilling it sideways. Over a table or a counter that is exactly the behaviour you want — what is lit is the surface itself, not the room around it.

The pierced body then adds a second layer to that focused light: whatever escapes sideways passes through the openings and lays a pattern of points across the wall. The same piece gives you task light and decorative light at once.

Two finishes are offered: antique brass and matte black. The ceiling mount is polished brass, which against a matte black body becomes a small but deliberate detail overhead.

The antique brass surface is not a plating but a hand-applied patina, and it darkens over time. The matte black is an electrostatic paint finish.

It suits living rooms, restaurants, hotel lobbies, corridors and kitchen islands, and set at even intervals along a counter it builds a steady rhythm.

Bulb not included. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours. Handmade in Istanbul.
