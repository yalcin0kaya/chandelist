Sculptural Side Table – Antique Brass Top, White Base

Price on request — request a quote.

A side table Ø35 cm across and 60 cm tall. The body is conical and sculptural, painted white; the top is antique brass. The division is deliberate: the white body carries no visual weight as it rises from the floor, while the brass top makes a warm point at eye level.

A single-colour table either dominates or disappears entirely. This two-material arrangement sits in between — the body stays quiet, the top is noticed.

The top is a comfortable surface for coffee, a book, a candle and small objects. Because the cone widens at the base it does not tip; it is stable despite looking slight.

The white surface is a custom paint finish, not a plating. The brass top is patinated by hand and darkens over time — so the two surfaces age at different rates, and the contrast between them grows with the years.

The metalwork and the surface finish are both done by hand in our Istanbul workshop.

It suits living rooms, bedrooms, reading corners, entrance halls and hotel lobbies. Available made to measure in other sizes and colours.
