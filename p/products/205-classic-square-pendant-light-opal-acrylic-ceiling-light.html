Classic Square Pendant Light – Opal Acrylic Ceiling Light

Price on request — request a quote.

A square pendant, 45 cm. The opal acrylic face hides the lamp completely and releases the light evenly from every point of the surface. Over a table people sit at, that matters: look up and you see an evenly lit plane, not a source that dazzles.

The square is a neutral form. It is not ornamented and it does not belong to a period, so it looks out of place neither in a modern kitchen nor in a classically furnished dining room. Where the lighting is not meant to set the character of the room, it is the right choice.

The metal body is made in white or black. White disappears into a pale ceiling, leaving only the lit face; black draws a defined frame against it.

It suits dining tables, kitchen islands, entrance halls, cafés and restaurants, and reads more strongly in runs of two or three over a long table.

Takes an E14 lamp holder; bulb not included.

Available made to measure in other sizes and colours, and in production runs for commercial projects. Made in our own Istanbul workshop.
