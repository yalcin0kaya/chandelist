Lotus Leaf Hammered Brass Chandelier – Hand-Chased Modern Pendant 40 cm

Price on request — request a quote.

Three leaves set at different heights on a single slim brass stem: a broad lotus pad above, a second leaf tilted slightly off-axis below it, a small cup at the base. None of them aligns with the others.

Every surface is chased by hand, stroke by stroke. Tens of thousands of chisel marks radiating from centre to rim give the brass the vein structure of an actual leaf. This is not a stamped or cast pattern — it is the record of the maker's hammer, and it differs on every piece.

The light source is never visible. The lamps sit beneath the leaves; what reaches the room has bounced off hammered brass first. That is why it is golden and diffuse rather than sharp — the chisel marks break it into thousands of small reflections.

50 cm wide, approximately 120 cm tall. The angle and spacing of the leaves are set to the room.

Antique brass patina. Handmade throughout.
