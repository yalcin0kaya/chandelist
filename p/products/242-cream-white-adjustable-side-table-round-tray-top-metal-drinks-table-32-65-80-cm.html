Cream White Adjustable Side Table – Round Tray Top Metal Drinks Table Ø32 × 65-80 cm

Price on request — request a quote.

A tray, a stem, a base. That is the whole table.

HEIGHT MECHANISM

The second rod running alongside the stem, with its two collars, is the adjustment. Loosen a collar, set the tray where you want it, tighten again. The table holds anywhere between 65 and 80 cm — no fixed steps, it locks wherever you leave it. Down to the arm of a sofa, up to the height you would stand and drink a coffee at. No tools needed.

THE TRAY

Ø32 cm, round. The rim turns up slightly — not decoration, but so a cup, a book or a glass will not slide off the edge. The surface is flat and needs no coaster.

THE BASE

Wide and weighted. Despite the Ø32 cm top the table does not tip; it stays steady even when leaned on. The base plate sits flush to the floor and holds firm on a rug.

COLOUR AND FINISH

Matt cream white. The colour is a purpose-made paint, not a plating. It will not wear through to another colour underneath and will not yellow with age. The surface does not hold fingerprints.

DIMENSIONS AND SPECIFICATION

Tray diameter: Ø32 cm

Base diameter: Ø32 cm

Height: adjustable, 65–80 cm

Weight: 3.5 kg

Material: Steel

Colour: Cream white (purpose-made paint)

Assembly: Flat-packed, screw assembly

MATERIAL AND ASSEMBLY

Body, tray and base are made of steel. The table ships flat-packed and is screw-assembled; tray, stem and base screw together in a few minutes. No extra tools required.

WHERE IT WORKS

Beside an armchair, at a bedside, in a reading corner, next to a desk, on a balcony or terrace, in a hotel room, café or lobby. Narrow enough to fit between furniture; adjustable enough to serve as both side table and drinks table.

CARE

Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners, steel wool or solvents. If used outdoors, it should not be left out in the rain.

MADE

In our own workshop. The same model is also available in gold and antique brass.
