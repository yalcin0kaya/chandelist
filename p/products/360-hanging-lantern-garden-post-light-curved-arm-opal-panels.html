Hanging Lantern Garden Post Light – Curved Arm, Opal Panels

Price on request — request a quote.

Instead of rising straight up, the post curves forward and carries the lantern clear of its own body. A small move, but it changes everything: the light falls where you walk, not at the foot of the post. Set at the edge of a path, this fitting lights the path and does not leave its own base in shadow.

The lantern hangs from a ring. It is not fixed; it sways gently in the wind and the light stays alive on the ground. Its four faces are closed with opal panels, and the pyramid roof throws rain clear and stops light escaping to the sky.

The opal panels never show the bulb from any angle. At night you see four bright rectangles with no glaring point inside. Along a path with several posts in a row that matters — the line of light stays even and does not tire the eye.

The post is a single piece, seated on a cone base that widens toward the ground. A floor flange and anchor set fix it into concrete or stone. The paint is a custom finish prepared for outdoor conditions: it will not rust or fade.

For garden paths, villa entrances, courtyards, hotel grounds and estate walkways. Post height and arm length are made to the project.

The same lantern head is also available on our straight-post model.
